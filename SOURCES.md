## Heritage designations and science
- UNESCO, Land of Frankincense: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1010 — World Heritage listing and the historic frankincense trade and its ports.
- UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, Al-Khanjar craft skills and social practices: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/al-khanjar-craft-skills-and-social-practices-01844 — documentation of the khanjar craft, its parts and materials.
- Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems, characterisation of Omani frankincense grades: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sustainable-food-systems/articles/10.3389/fsufs.2026.1785309/full — grades (Hojari, Najdi, Sha'bi) and growing-region differences.
- CITES, report on incense species: https://cites.org/sites/default/files/documents/E-CoP19-Inf-10-R1.pdf — reference point that frankincense species were not listed under CITES at the time of the report; national controls still apply.
- IJETRM, conservation of tangible heritage in Oman — Mutrah Souq: https://ijetrm.com/issues/files/Feb-2024-08-1707380171-FEB003.pdf — architectural analysis of the market, its entrances and the use of timber in roofs and arches.
- MDPI Sustainability, form and function in two traditional Middle Eastern souqs: https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/sustainability/sustainability-12-07154/article_deploy/sustainability-12-07154.pdf — architectural comparison including Mutrah Souq.
